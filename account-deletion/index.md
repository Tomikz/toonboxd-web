# Delete your Toonboxd account

*Version française : [Supprimer ton compte Toonboxd](https://toonboxd.app/suppression-compte/).*

Toonboxd is an app for tracking your reading of manhwa, manhua and webtoons, published by Paul Maxime Nadin, self-employed (French SIREN 991987819), France. This page says how to delete your Toonboxd account, what goes and what stays. It repeats the [privacy policy](https://toonboxd.app/privacy/), whose French version prevails.

## From the app

1. Open the "Profile" tab, then "Settings" (the gear).
2. At the very bottom, tap "Delete Account".
3. A screen says what gets deleted and what stays. Tap the "Delete Account" button.
4. The app asks "Delete Your Account?". Tap "Delete".
5. The app shows "Your account is deleted", with the user ID it had. Write it down if you want to ask us to erase your analytics data.

## Without the app

If you have deleted the app, write to contact@toonboxd.app with your user ID, and ask us to delete your account. The user ID is shown in the app: Settings, Account section, "Your User ID". Without it, we cannot find you: an anonymous account has nothing else. We answer within a month.

## What goes, right away and for good

Everything Toonboxd stores about you: your account, your library, your reading history, your ratings, your pause notes, your username, your time zone, your card label, your reports, the notification token of your devices, your beta tester date if you had one, and the Apple address if you had linked your account. Your additions and ratings leave the Explore rankings at the next recalculation, within an hour.

## What stays

- Your title suggestions, with nothing left that links them to you: they help us build the list of series.
- Your subscription, if you have one. It is managed in your Apple settings, and deleting your account does not cancel it.
- The link to your Apple Account, on Apple's side. You remove it yourself: Settings, your name, Sign in with Apple, then Toonboxd. The app reminds you when you delete your account, with a link to Apple's help page.
- At Expo, what it received to deliver your notifications. We do not know yet what it keeps.

## What deleting does not do

It does not erase what was already sent to the analytics services. PostHog and Firebase keep the events they received, filed under your ID. To erase them:

1. Write down your user ID. The app shows it in Settings ("Your User ID"), and the screen that confirms the deletion shows it one last time.
2. Send it to contact@toonboxd.app and ask for erasure.
3. We ask PostHog and Firebase for erasure within the following month. Each service then handles the request at its own pace, which we do not control.

At Firebase Analytics, data is kept for two months.

Sentry has nothing that points to you, so there is nothing to erase person by person. RevenueCat files your subscription under your account ID: you can ask us to erase it the same way, with this ID, once the subscription has ended; the subscription itself stays with Apple, and you cancel it in your Apple settings.

## How long we keep your data

As long as your account exists. Nothing is erased automatically: you decide, with the button described above. One known exception: if you use "Recover My Library" to get back an account linked to Apple, the anonymous account you leave stays in our database, with what it contained and with nothing that could identify you. An automatic purge of these accounts is planned.

For any question: contact@toonboxd.app. The details are in the [privacy policy](https://toonboxd.app/privacy/).
