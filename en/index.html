Frequently asked questions

Another question? Write to contact@toonboxd.app.

What is Toonboxd?

Toonboxd is a manhwa tracker and webtoon tracker for iPhone, in English and French. You sort your series by status, move forward chapter by chapter, write a note when you put a series on hold, and the app lets you know when it’s back. The idea fits in a few words: Letterboxd for manhwa.

What is a manhwa tracker?

A manhwa tracker is an app that remembers where you are in each of your manhwa (Korean comics): the last chapter you read, its status, your rating. No more keeping a list in your notes or rereading chapters to find your place. Toonboxd does the same for manhua and webtoons.

Is there a webtoon tracker in English?

Yes: Toonboxd is a webtoon tracker in English and French, coming to iPhone in the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Ireland, New Zealand and France. It lists nearly 9,700 vertical manhwa, manhua and webtoons.

How do I know when a manhwa on hiatus is back?

Put it on hold in Toonboxd. When a reader reports that it’s back and the report is confirmed, everyone who had it on hold gets a notification within five minutes, and one tap opens its page.

How do I share my top 5 manhwa?

Put together your top 5 in Toonboxd: the eye on your top 5, in your profile, opens your card. Your #1 series up front with your rating and your chapter, the next four below, your total chapters read, in story format. You can swap the “My top 5 manhwa” label for your own, for free.

Is Toonboxd free?

Yes, with no ads. The free version tracks up to 30 series you’re reading, with no limit on series in Plan to Read, On Hold, Completed or Dropped, and three editable lists. Toonboxd Pro is yearly or monthly, with 7 days free on both plans with a first subscription. The App Store shows the price in your currency.

Can I read chapters in the app?

No. Toonboxd hosts no chapters. The “Read on…” button on every series page opens the official platform where you can read it.

Do I need to create an account?

No. The app creates an anonymous account, with no email or password. You can link it to your Apple Account to get your library back on another iPhone, and delete it entirely from Settings.

Does Toonboxd have Japanese manga?

No, and that’s on purpose: Toonboxd tracks manhwa, manhua and webtoons. No Japanese manga. If a series like these is missing, you can suggest it from Search, for free.

Is Toonboxd on Android?

Android is next: Toonboxd comes to iPhone first, and the Android version follows. Choose Android when you sign up for the launch: we’ll write to you for the beta, then on the day it arrives on your phone.

When does Toonboxd come out?

Toonboxd is coming soon to the App Store, and to Android after that. To hear about it, leave your email and choose your phone: we’ll write to you for the beta, then on the day Toonboxd arrives on your phone. Nothing else.
